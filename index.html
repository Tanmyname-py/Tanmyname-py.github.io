Light Music Club Members

Hōkago Tea Time

Meet the talented members of our band!

Mio Akiyama

Bassist & Vocalist

The mature and responsible bassist of the band. Despite being left-handed, she plays a right-handed bass. Easily frightened but extremely talented and dedicated to music.

Ritsu Tainaka

Drummer & President

The energetic club president and drummer. Always cheerful and upbeat, she's the one who convinced everyone to join the Light Music Club. Best friends with Mio since childhood.

Tsumugi Kotobuki

Keyboardist

The gentle and wealthy keyboardist, known as "Mugi". Always brings delicious snacks and tea for the club. Her keyboard skills are exceptional, and she's fascinated by common experiences.

Azusa Nakano

Rhythm Guitarist

The talented underclassman who joined later. A serious musician who balances the club's playful nature. Despite her initial skepticism, she grows to love the Light Music Club deeply.
